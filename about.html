About · Phoenix & Glendale

Built for the stage — and the families in the house

Gotham Arts HD is a Glendale video production company serving Phoenix and the Valley, with dance recital films at the center and live event and corporate work alongside.

The company

High-definition films from Glendale, for the Valley

From our Glendale base we film dance recitals, live events, product demos, corporate programs, and meeting or legal sessions. Studios trust the no-set-up-fee recital model, the studio commission, and a three-to-four-week turnaround. Producers call when they need a crew that can move from a theater lobby to a conference floor without losing polish.

Our work has taken us from Valley venues like the Scottsdale Center for the Arts to stages as large as Radio City Music Hall — and to dance communities from Times Square to Seattle, Jacksonville to Houston.

Leadership

Bill Hotaling

Bill Hotaling leads Gotham Arts HD with a dancer’s eye for the stage and a producer’s discipline on show day. That mix is why Valley studios rely on the crew for long recital weekends — and why event clients trust the same team when the room is packed and the schedule is tight.

“Every recital is a family’s keepsake and a studio’s reputation. We show up ready, stay invisible during the show, and deliver a film worth watching again.”

Background

Bill began his dance training in New York City and has performed on stages from Radio City Music Hall to the World Design Expo in Japan. His credits include music videos, industrials for companies such as AT&T and IBM, commercials, and an appearance in the film When Harry Met Sally.

Teaching and choreography have been a long-running focus — including work at Broadway Dance Center, Steps on Broadway, and Central Park Dance Studio, and choreography for theatrical productions such as Chicago, The Music Man, Fiddler on the Roof, and Pippin.

He has taught workshops and master classes for organizations including the Manhattan Dance Project (which he founded), Dance Masters of America, and Dance Caravan; for colleges and universities; and at studios and festivals around the world. Styles include jazz, lyrical, theatre dance, hip-hop, and swing jazz.

How we work

What you can expect on a Gotham Arts HD job

  • Clear planning

    We confirm cues, feeds, and delivery details before the curtain rises — so show day is execution, not improvisation.

  • Quiet on the floor

    Cameras and crew stay out of the dancers’ way and out of the audience’s sightline.

  • Finish that holds up

    Professional non-linear editing, digital family plans, and optional DVD or Blu-ray packaging.
